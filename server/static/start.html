CleanScape

Community clean-ups, with Earthy, an AI helper you can talk to. It opens right here in your browser. Nothing to install.

Open CleanScape in browser

To try Earthy

  • Tap the round Earthy button, then a question, to hear it answer.
  • Tap the scan button to show Earthy an item, or use a sample photo.
  • Say yes when the browser asks for the microphone or camera.

If the microphone or camera does not work, open this page in Safari or Chrome instead of inside another app.

On Android, there is also the installed version

It runs inside Expo Go, a free app. Install Expo Go, then come back and open the app in it.
